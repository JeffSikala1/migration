From what we’ve reviewed, JFrog recommends using OIDC/OAuth for Artifactory integrations rather than SAML because it works better with modern token-based authentication and CI/CD automation workflows. That was the main reason we initially pursued the OAuth configuration. That said, we want to align with your team’s security requirements, so if PKCE is required or SAML is the preferred standard in your environment we can support either approach. At the moment OAuth is functioning after PKCE was disabled, but we’re open to adjusting the configuration if needed. Please let us know which direction your team would prefer us to standardize on.
