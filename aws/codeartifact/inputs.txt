the var segment in /opt/jfrog/artifactory/var/... is a symlink to the mount root itself (/var/opt/jfrog/artifactory), not a subdirectory under it. So any config path referenced as .../var/etc/system.yaml inside the container actually lands at <mount-root>/etc/system.yaml on the host, not <mount-root>/var/etc/system.yaml.
