We don’t have a strict preference as long as the method aligns with your security requirements. We initially used OIDC/OAuth because it integrates well with Artifactory and works well with token-based workflows used by CI/CD and automation, but if PKCE is required on your side we’re open to adjusting the configuration. If SAML or an OIDC configuration with PKCE is the preferred standard for your environment, we can support either approach.
