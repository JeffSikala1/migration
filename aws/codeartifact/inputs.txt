Hey Bon. So I took a look at the backup script again. There’s nothing in there that actually generates secrets for S3. What it’s doing is backing up existing data and pushing it to the bucket. That said, some of the backups definitely contain sensitive material thus why they are in the secrets folder. Specifically, anything under the NGINX/TLS backup (like /etc/ssl, /etc/pki, nginx configs) and the Bamboo volumes/keystores could include certs, private keys, or other encrypted credentials. Those get tarred up and uploaded — so the secrets folder in S3, it’s likely just those archived artifacts being organized that way, not something dynamically created by the script. The metadata file is just inventory info (containers, volumes, timestamps etc), nothing secret related there. S3 is just the storage target the actual secrets originate from the host/containers, and this job is simply backing them up.
